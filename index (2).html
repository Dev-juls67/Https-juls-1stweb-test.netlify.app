Curricula designed for innovation, leadership, and professional excellence.

Technology

BSIT

BSIT is more than just learning about computers and technology; it is about developing creativity, problem-solving skills, and innovation. As future IT professionals, we strive to use technology to create solutions, connect people, and make a positive impact on society. With knowledge, determination, and passion, we are ready to face the challenges of a constantly changing digital world.

Software Dev Networking Cloud & AI
🧑🏻‍🍳
Hospitality

HM

“Serve with Heart, Lead with Excellence.” Hospitality Management is not only about providing excellent service, but also about creating meaningful experiences for every guest. With passion, respect, and dedication, we strive to welcome others with warmth, handle every challenge with professionalism, and make every service memorable. As future hospitality professionals, we aim to serve with a genuine heart and lead with excellence wherever our careers take us.

Culinary Arts Tourism Guest Relations
🤵🏻
Business

BSBA

“Lead with Purpose, Succeed with Integrity.” BSBA is about developing strong leadership, decision-making, and business skills to prepare students for the challenges of the business world. We strive to become responsible and innovative professionals who value hard work, honesty, and teamwork. With determination and a passion for success, we aim to create opportunities, inspire others, and contribute positively to the growth of businesses and communities.

Entrepreneurship Marketing Finance
🧑🏻‍🏫
Education

BEED

“Inspire Young Minds, Shape Bright Futures.” BEED is more than learning how to teach; it is about inspiring children to discover their potential and guiding them toward a brighter future. As future educators, we strive to teach with patience, compassion, and dedication while creating a positive and supportive learning environment. With knowledge, passion, and a heart for service, we aim to make a meaningful difference in the lives of every learner we teach.

Child Pedagogy Instruction Mentorship
🧑🏻‍🌾
Agriculture

AGRICULTURE

“Cultivate Today, Harvest a Better Tomorrow.” Agriculture is more than growing crops and raising animals; it is about nurturing the land, supporting communities, and providing food for future generations. As future agricultural professionals, we strive to promote sustainable farming, care for our natural resources, and use knowledge and innovation to improve agricultural practices. With hard work, passion, and respect for nature, we aim to contribute to a stronger and more sustainable future.

Crop Science Sustainability Agri-Business
🧑🏻‍💼
Social Work

BSSW

“Serve with Compassion, Empower with Purpose.” BSSW is more than helping people through difficult situations; it is about understanding individuals, supporting families, and empowering communities to create positive change. As future social workers, we strive to serve with compassion, respect, and dedication while promoting equality, dignity, and social justice. With knowledge, empathy, and a commitment to service, we aim to make a meaningful difference in the lives of others and help build stronger communities.

Community Action Family Care Advocacy
